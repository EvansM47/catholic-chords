TITLE: UTUSHUKIE ROHO
BOOK_NUMBER: 601
CATEGORY: HOLY SPIRIT

VERSE 1:
Utushukie roho
Kama ndimi za moto,
kama upepo uvumavyo,
chemichemi ya uzima
Wewe ni Mungu wetu,
roho Mtakatifu,
wewe ni Mumba wetu,
sisi ni wana wako,
Na kualika Mungu Roho
mwili wangu hekalu lako,
nizaematunda mema,
nishuhudie wema wako.
Shuka na upendo wako,
shuka na furaha yako
shuka na amani yako,
ili tuwe watakatifu.
Utuongezee Imani,
na matumaini kwako,
utuongezee ukarimu,
ili tuwe watakatifu.
