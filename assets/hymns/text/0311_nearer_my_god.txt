HYMN_NUMBER: 311
TITLE: NEARER MY GOD

VERSE 1:
Nearer, my God, to Thee, nearer to Thee
Ev’n though it be a cross that raiseth me,
Still all my song shall be,
Nearer, my God, to Thee, nearer to thee
Deep in Thy sacred heart let me abide
Thou who hast bled for me
sorrowed and died
Sweet shall my weeping be,
grief surely leading me
Nearer, my God, to Thee, nearer to Thee
Friends may depart from me,
night may come down
Clouds of adversity darken and frown
Still through my tears I’ll see
hope gently leading me
Nearer, my God, to Thee, nearer to Thee
And when the goal is won,
how like a dream
In the dim retrospect, sorrow will seem
Sweet will my transport be
Jesus, thy face to see
When I have come at last, nearer to Thee
