TITLE: ALLELUIA JESUS WE WORSHIP YOU

VERSE 1:
You paid my ransom,

You who died so I get healed,

You became a sacrifice Jesus I worship you
All the nations they worship,

You who was and is to come,

You paid my ransom, Jesus I worship you

On which you died for me,

Oh I see your great love,

Your great unending love.

When I gaze at your wounded side,
And I see the crown of thorns,
I am filled with love for you,
I worship you my Lord.
