HYMN_NUMBER: 363
TITLE: HAIL QUEEN OF HEAVEN

VERSE 1:
Hail Queen of heaven, the ocean star,
Guide of the wand’rer here below
Thrown on life’s surge, we claim thy care,
Save us from peril and from woe.
Mother of Christ, star of the sea, pray
for the wand’rer, pray for me.
O gentle, chaste and spotless maid, We
sinners make our prayers through thee.
Remind thy Son that he has paid. The price
of our iniquity. Virgin most pure, star of the
sea, pray for the sinner, pray for me.
And while to him who reigns above,
in Godhead one, in person three, the
source of life, of grace, of love, homage
we pay on bended knee. Do thou bright
Queen, star of the sea, pray for thy
children pray for me.
