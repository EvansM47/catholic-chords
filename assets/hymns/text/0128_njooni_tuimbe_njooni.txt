HYMN_NUMBER: 128
TITLE: NJOONI TUIMBE NJOONI

VERSE 1:
**TUCHEZE**
***Njooni tuimbe, njooni tucheze, njooni***
***tufurahi mbele zake Bwana. Piga makofi,***
***piga vigelegele shangilieni Bwana leo asubuhi.***
Sauti ya kwanza semeni alelluya

Sauti ya pili tumsifu Yesu Kristu.

Sauti ya tatu na ile ya nne

waseme pamoja milele amina.
Wa mama wote pigeni vigelegele
Wababa wote vinanda vipigwe sana.
Pigeni filimbi, ngoma na kayamba.
Watoto wadogo wachangamkeni.
Mataifa yote njooni mbele za Bwana.
Makabila yote simameni tuimbe.
Nyanyukeni nyote pigeni makofi,
shangwe, nderemo, vifijo machezo.
Bahari na vitu vyote vivijazavyo.

Milima, mito na miti shangilieni.

Ndege wa angani na warukeruke,
wanyama kondeni furahini sana.
