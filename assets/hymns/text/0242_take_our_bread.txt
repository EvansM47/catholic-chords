HYMN_NUMBER: 242
TITLE: TAKE OUR BREAD

VERSE 1:
***Take our bread, we ask you,***
***take our hearts, we love you,***
***take our lives, oh Father,***
***we are yours, we are yours.***
Yours as we stand at the table you set,
Yours as we eat the bread our hearts
can’t forget. We are the signs of your
life with us yet; we are yours, we are yours.
Your holy people stand washed in your blood,
Spirit filled, yet hungry, we await your food.
Poor thought we are,
we have brought Ourselves to you:
We are yours, we are yours.
