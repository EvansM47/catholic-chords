HYMN_NUMBER: 39
TITLE: GOD WILL MAKE A WAY

VERSE 1:
God will make a way, where there
seems to be no way. He works in ways
that we cannot see, He will make a way
for me, He will be my guide hold me
closely to His side, with love and strength
for each new day, He will make a way,
He will make a way
By a roadway in the wilderness
He’ll lead me and rivers in the desert
will I see, Heaven and earth will fade
away but His Word will still remain and
He will do something new today
