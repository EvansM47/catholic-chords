HYMN_NUMBER: 815
TITLE: CHRIST THE LORD IS RISEN
BOOK_NUMBER: 688
CATEGORY: EASTER HYMNS

VERSE 1:
Christ the Lord is risen today, Alleluia! Earth and heaven in chorus say, Alleluia!
Sing, ye heavens, and earth reply, Alleluia! Raise your joys and triumphs high, Alleluia!
Fought the fight, the battle won, Alleluia! Love's redeeming work is done, Alleluia!
Death in vain forbids him rise, Alleluia!
Christ has opened paradise, Alleluia!
Lives again our glorious King, Alleluia! Where, O death, is now thy sting? Alleluia!
Once he died our souls to save, Alleluia! Wheres thy victory, boasting grave? Alleluia!
Soar we now where Christ has led, Alleluia!
Following our exalted Head, Alleluia! Made like him, like him we rise, Alleluia!

**-ALLGLORYTOJESUS**
