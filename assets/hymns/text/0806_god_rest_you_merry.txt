HYMN_NUMBER: 806
TITLE: GOD REST YOU MERRY
BOOK_NUMBER: 652
CATEGORY: CHRISTMAS HYMNS

VERSE 1:
God rest you merry, gentle men,
let nothing you dismay,
remember Christ our Savior
was born on Christmas day,
to save us all from Satan's pow'r
when we were gone astray;
O tidings of comfort and joy,
comfort and joy,
O tidings of comfort and joy.
From God our heav'nly Father
a blessed angel came; and unto certain
shepherds, brought tidings of the same:
how that in Bethlehem was born
the son of God by name
Fear not, then, said the angel, let nothing you affright; this day is born a
Savior, of a pure virgin bright,
to free all those who trust in him
from Satan's pow'r and might.
The shepherds at those tidings
rejoiced much in mind, and left their flocks a-feeding,
in tempest, storm, and wind, and went to Bethlehem straightway,
the Son of God to find.
