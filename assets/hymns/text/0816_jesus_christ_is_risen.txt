HYMN_NUMBER: 816
TITLE: JESUS CHRIST IS RISEN
BOOK_NUMBER: 689
CATEGORY: EASTER HYMNS

VERSE 1:
our triumphant holy day, Alleluia! Jesus Christ is ris'n today, Alleluia!
suffer to redeem our loss. Alleluia! who did once upon the cross Alleluia!
unto Christ our heav'nly King, Alleluia! Hymns of praise then let us sing Alleluia!
sinners to redeem and save. Alleluia! who endured the cross and grave, Alleluia!
But the pains which he endured, Alleluia!
our salvation have procured; Alleluia!
now above the sky he's King, Alleluia!
where the angels ever sing. Alleluia!
Sing we to our God above Alleluia!
praise eternal as his love, Alleluia!
praise him, all ye heav'nly host, Alleluia!
Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. Alleluia!
