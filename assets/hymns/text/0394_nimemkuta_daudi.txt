HYMN_NUMBER: 394
TITLE: NIMEMKUTA DAUDI

VERSE 1:
***Nimemkuta Daudi mtumishi wangu***
***mwema, nikampaka mafuta ili mkono***
***wangu uwe naye daima x2***
Mkono wangu uliomtia mafuta ndio
utakaomtilia nguvu. Ili adui asipate
kumdhuru, wala watesi wake wasipate
kumtesa.
Kwa mkono wangu hodari, pembe yake
itatukuka sana. Daima ataniita Mungu
wangu, nami nitamuita mwanangu.
