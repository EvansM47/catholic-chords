HYMN_NUMBER: 433
TITLE: UNIONDOLEE DHAMBI

VERSE 1:
***Uniondolee dhambi, nitakate,***
***unioshe niwe mweupe pe x2***
Nakiri makosa yangu,
naziona dhambi zangu,
unioshe niwe mweupe pe
Mimi kweli mkosefu,
tangu kuzaliwa kwangu,
mwenye dhambi tangu tumboni
mwa mama yangu
Hivyo unafanya vyema,
wewe unaponihukumi,
una haki unaponiadhibu
Wewe wataka unyofu,
ule unyofu wa ndani,
nifundishe hekima moyoni
Niumbie moyo safi,
na uweke ndani yangu,
roho mpya iliyo thabiti.
