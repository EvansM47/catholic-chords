HYMN_NUMBER: 365
TITLE: IMMACULATE MARY

VERSE 1:
Immaculate Mary, your praises we sing.
Who reignest with Christ
our Redeemer and King.
***Ave ave ave Maria***
***Ave ave ave Maria***
In Heaven the blessed your glory
proclaim, on earth we your children
invoke your sweet name
We pray you o mother may God’s will
be done, we pray for His glory
may His kingdom come
We pray for our Mother, the church upon
earth. And bless, Holy Mary,
the land of our birth.
