TITLE: HEART OF CHRIST

VERSE 1:
Heart of Christ source of light for all the
world. Only guide for us in life today
Show us Lord the path we are to take
Heart of light, you are the way.
Sacred heart, source of hope to end
despair. Give us courage and the
strength of youth. As we bring your
message to the world. Heart of hope,
you are the truth.
Heart of God source of love, we are to
share. Let its power bring calm where
there is strife. As we seek our destiny in
you. Hearts of God, for you are life.
You O Lord are the way, the truth, the
life. On our way, your truth will never
cease. There is life that you alone can
give. Hearts of love, for you are peace.
