HYMN_NUMBER: 319
TITLE: SOUL OF MY SAVIOUR

VERSE 1:
Soul of my Savior sanctify my breast,
body of Christ, be thou my saving guest,
Blood of my Savior, bathe me in thy tide,
wash me with waters gushing from thy side.
Strength and protection may thy passion be,
o blessed Jesus, hear and answer me;
deep in thy wounds, Lord, hide and
shelter me, so shall I never,
never part from thee.
Guard and defend me from the foe
malign, in death’s dread moments make
me only thine; call me and bid me come
to thee on high where I may praise thee
with thy saints for ay.
