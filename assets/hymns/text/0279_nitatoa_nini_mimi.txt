HYMN_NUMBER: 279
TITLE: NITATOA NINI MIMI

VERSE 1:
***Nitatoa nini mimi ewe Mungu wangu,***
***kitakachokuwa sawa na fadhili zako,***
***wewe ni Bwana (wa rehema), fadhili***
***zako (za milele), nikupe nini (nikupe)***
***nikushukuru. Kama ni fedha (nazo ni zako)***
***hata mazao (ya mashamba) nikupe nini***
***(nikupe) nikushukuru***
Mema mengi umenitendea eeh Mungu
wangu, hata siwezi kukulipa, mara ngapi
mimekukosea eeh Mungu wangu na
wewe ukanisamehe.
Usiku hata mchana wewe unanilinda,
hata hivyo sikukushukuru, umenilisha
chakula ili nipate nguvu lakini
sikukushukuru
ninajitolea kwako kimwili na kiroho,
vyote hata maisha yangu, nitaimba
nikisema asante Mungu wangu, siku zote
hata milele
Nakutolea mkate ni mmea wa ngano,
divai tunda la mzabibu, kwa fumbo la
maji haya pia divai hii nishiriki umungu
wako.
