HYMN_NUMBER: 195
TITLE: UBI CARITAS

VERSE 1:
***Ubi caritas et amor ubi caritas, Deus ibiest***
If I have the gift of prophecy,
understanding all the mysteries there are
knowing everything, if I have faith in all
its fullness to move mountain but have no love,
I am nothing at all
If I give everything I have to feed the
poor, and let them take my body to be
burned but have no love,
I gain nothing at all
Love is patient, love is not jealous, love
does not rejoice in what is wrong but
love rejoices in the truth. Love is always
ready to excuse, to hope to trust and to
endure whatever comes
Love never fails, prophecies will cease
and tongues will be silent, knowledge
will pass away: but there are only three
things in the end that last; faith, hope
and love and the greatest of these is love
