HYMN_NUMBER: 119
TITLE: I COME TO YOU LORD

VERSE 1:
**(NIMEINGIA ENGLISH)**
***I come to you Lord (my God), In this Holy***
***house to praise and honour to praise and***
***honour (you Lord), and thank you my God***
***is all I can do***
1. My thank to you Lord for this new
day of my life, I ask for good health
and my salvation
2. Enter rejoice and come in brothers
and sisters, for this is the day of
praise and honour
3. I ask for mercy for I am a sinner, I
come to you God so purify me
4. For adoration and for glorification, I
come to you Lord listen to my prayer
5. You are the good Lord and so I
beseech you, protect me this day
and don’t forsake me
