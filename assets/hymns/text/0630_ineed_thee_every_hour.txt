TITLE: INEED THEE EVERY HOUR
BOOK_NUMBER: 242
CATEGORY: COMMUNION

VERSE 1:
I need Thee every hour,
Most gracious Lord;
No tender voice like Thine
Can peace afford.
I need Thee, oh, I need Thee;
Every hour I need Thee;
Oh, bless menow, my Savior!
I come to Thee.
I need Thee every hour,
Stay Thou near by;
Temptations lose their power
When Thou art nigh.
I need Thee every hour,
In joy or pain;
Come quickly and abide,
Or life is vain.

Often, grievously
I have sinned against Thee, That Thou comet to me?
Who am I, my Jesus,
As my King and Lord.
Kneeling, Iadore Thee
On Thy only word;
Jesus, Ibelieve it
To my sinful heart.
Thou, the God who made me,
Holy as Thou art,
Jesus, Thou art coming,
