HYMN_NUMBER: 131
TITLE: O THE LOVE OF MY LORD IS THE

VERSE 1:
**ESSENCE**
O the love of my Lord is the essence,
of all that I love here on earth. All the
beauty I see he has given to me and his
giving is gentle as silence
Every day every hour every moment,
has been blessed by the strength of his love.
At the turn of each tide he is there at my
side and his touch is as gentle as silence
There’ve been times when I’ve turned
from his presence, and I’ve walked other
paths,other ways but I’ve called on his
name in the dark of my shame and his
mercy was gentle as silence
