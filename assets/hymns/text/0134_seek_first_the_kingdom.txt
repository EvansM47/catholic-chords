HYMN_NUMBER: 134
TITLE: SEEK FIRST THE KINGDOM

VERSE 1:
***Seek first the kingdom of God***
***And all the rest will fall in line***
***Give God His due and God will give back***
***to you, turn your water into wine.***
Some men are money-mad, go about
richly clad, seeking the rainbow’s end.

I tell you, God will surely repay, all
those who give away, to anyone in need.
Some men are barely fed, begging their
crust of bread, from dawn to setting sun.
I tell you God will give liberty, to those
in poverty. A feast to everyone.
Some men are neither nor, life rushes by
their door, leaving them far behind. I tell
you, jump in and join the fight, stand up
for what is right. Serve God in mankind.
